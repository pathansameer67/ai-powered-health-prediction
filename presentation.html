The Problem

Why Current Healthcare AI Chatbots Fail

❌ Hallucinations & Fake Dosages

Generic LLMs guess medical facts and fabricate drug dosages without grounding against verified medical literature.

⚠️ Medical Jargon & Panic

Dense clinical terms (e.g. "idiopathic infiltration") confuse and terrify everyday patients instead of offering actionable guidance.

🚨 Missed Emergency Red Flags

Single-prompt chatbots fail to catch cardiac, stroke, and anaphylaxis signs, treating emergencies like standard inquiries.

👁️ No X-Ray / Image Interpretation

Patients with X-Rays or lab scans cannot get automated preliminary imaging reviews or fracture/pneumonia triage.

Clinical Safety

Three-Tier Safety & Escalation Framework

🚨
Category A: Immediate Life-Threatening Emergency

Triggers instant interception before text generation. Displays local emergency numbers (911 / 112) and first-aid steps for chest pain, stroke, or severe trauma.

⚠️
Category B: Red-Flag Clinical Warning

Provides safe educational guidance while prominently showing a medical consultation banner and specific questions to ask a doctor (e.g. fever >103°F, unexplained weight loss).

✅
Category C: Safe General Health Guidance

Generates full structured 5-section plain-language guidance for common everyday issues (fever self-care, BP benchmarks, acid reflux diet).

Patient Clarity

Universal 5-Section Response Format

📋 1. Direct Answer in Plain Words

Clear, empathetic 2-3 sentence overview answering the question directly.

🔍 2. What Is Happening & Common Causes

Biological mechanisms and normal ranges/benchmarks in bold.

💡 3. Safe Home Care & Practical Steps

Hydration, resting, symptom monitoring, and doctor consultation questions.

⚠️ 4. Warning Signs (When to See a Doctor)

Clear bulleted red flags requiring prompt medical evaluation.

📖 5. Medical Terms Made Simple

1-2 key clinical terms translated into everyday vocabulary (e.g., Systolic, LES, Antipyretic).
